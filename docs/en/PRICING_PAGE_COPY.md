# Sovereign Coder Pricing

Last updated: 2026-04-04

## Built For Makers And Teams

Start free, scale when your organization needs more power.

- Individuals: Free forever
- Organizations (up to 5 users): Free
- Organizations (6+ users): Free growth tier with usage limits, or upgrade for more capacity and enterprise controls

## Plans At A Glance

| Plan | Who It's For | Price | Key Limits / Features |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Individual Free | Personal developers | $0 | Core product access, community support |
| Organization Starter Free | Teams with 1-5 users | $0 | Shared workspace, basic collaboration |
| Organization Growth Free | Teams with 6+ users testing at scale | $0 | Usage-limited API/automation, no SSO/SCIM/compliance exports |
| Pro | Growing teams (6-50 users) | $5/user/month | Higher quotas, priority support, advanced team controls |
| Business | Mid-size teams (51-200 users) | $7/user/month | Advanced analytics, audit capabilities, broader integrations |
| Enterprise | Large organizations (201+ users) | $9/user/month or custom annual | SSO/SAML, SCIM, compliance controls, enterprise support |

## Why We Offer A Free Growth Tier

We optimize for adoption in year one.

If your organization grows beyond 5 users, you can keep using Sovereign Coder for free with capacity limits. Upgrade only when you need:

- More automation/API capacity
- Advanced security and compliance
- Enterprise admin and identity features
- Higher support SLA

## Annual Billing

- Monthly billing available for all paid plans
- Annual billing: 10% discount

## How Upgrades Work

We never hard-stop your team without warning.

- In-product alerts at 70%, 85%, and 100% of free growth limits
- Optional 14-day temporary quota boost on first limit hit
- Seamless upgrade with prorated seat billing

## Seat Billing Basics

- Pricing is per active user seat
- Seats are prorated when added or removed mid-cycle
- Paid plans begin when you choose to upgrade from free growth limits

## FAQ

### Is Sovereign Coder really free for individuals?
Yes. Individual accounts are free forever.

### Is it free for small organizations?
Yes. Organizations with up to 5 users are free.

### What happens when we add a 6th user?
You enter Organization Growth Free (still $0) with usage limits. You can stay there or upgrade to Pro.

### When should we move to paid?
Most teams upgrade when they need higher quotas, enterprise security features, or stronger support guarantees.

### Do you support enterprise procurement?
Yes. Enterprise plan supports invoicing and custom annual contracts.
